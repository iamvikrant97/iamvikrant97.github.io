The data

  • The CTR beat what I expected. Search ads for a niche service usually sit around 3 to 5%. 14.51% CTR is high for search. The keywords matched what people were actually searching for.
  • Cost per click held steady across the run, roughly ₹12 to ₹15 a day. No spikes, no wasted spend chasing broad terms.
  • One term did most of the work. "Photographer near me" pulled 10 of the 118 clicks on its own, at a 30% CTR. No other term came close.
  • Search intent was hyperlocal. Beyond "Mumbai," people searched by neighbourhood: Thane, Kalyan, Ghatkopar, Borivali, Panvel. Next round, I'd build separate ad groups for those areas instead of running one broad campaign.
Note. The ad ran for six days on a small budget. Keywords, budget, and results, all collected directly from Google Ads.
