Jobscan.co — ATS Score Checker

Paste your CV + job description. Shows missing keywords and ATS match score. Free for 5 scans/month. Do this before every MNC application.

jobscan.co → paste CV left → paste JD right → scan → add missing keywords you can honestly claim

Claude — Tailor CV per job

Paste any JD and Claude rewrites your CV bullets to match it without adding false experience.

"Here is a job description: [paste JD]. Here is my CV: [paste CV]. Rewrite my bullets to match this JD without adding experience I don't have. Keep language honest and direct, not AI-sounding."

Claude — Cover letter per application
